    Connect your Spotify account

    Needed to import reference and comparison playlists and to export the finished playlist. Without connecting, you can still build the playlist and play previews.

    Who are you, and what will the playlist be called?

    Your name appears as the author of the tracks you add, next to the names of the people who help. You can change both later.

    What is the style of the playlist?

    Describe it in your own words: genre, era, mood, occasion. Suggestions will follow this style. Optional.

    Start from a reference playlist?

    Paste the link to a Spotify playlist (yours or collaborative), a Deezer playlist (any public one) or a YouTube playlist. It is the basis for suggestions and for the diff. Optional.

    Spotify links require signing in; Deezer and YouTube links work without connecting.

    Compare with other playlists?

    Add as many as you like. Tracks that appear in several of them show what they have in common. Spotify only allows your own or collaborative playlists; for other people's, use a Deezer or YouTube link. Optional.

    Spotify links require signing in; Deezer and YouTube links work without connecting.

                        Share playlist

                        Generate a link and a QR code so friends can add tracks from their phones. They only enter a name, and you see who added each track and when.

                        Works for anyone on the same Wi-Fi network as this computer. Whoever opens the link only sees this playlist: they cannot reach the rest of the app, your Spotify or other playlists. You can stop sharing at any time.

                        Ask them to point their phone camera at the QR code.

                        Hidden tracks

                        They will not come back in this playlist's suggestions. If you hide 2 tracks by the same artist, the whole artist is removed from suggestions.
